How to read this shelf

The bar is deliberately awkward. A saved article is a intention; a highlighted one is evidence. Anything opened and abandoned in the first quarter stays off the shelf entirely — which is why the shelf is much shorter than the library behind it (1945 documents synced).

Progress percentages on PDFs and videos are Readwise's scroll estimate, so 100% on a talk means the transcript reached the end, not that every minute was watched. Highlight counts are the harder evidence.
