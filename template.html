How to read this shelf

The bar is deliberately awkward. A saved article is an intention; a highlighted one is evidence. The shelf starts at the lowest honest setting — anything you opened and got 5% into — and the slider lets you raise it. Push it to 50% and you are asking a much harder question: what did I actually finish reading? Either way it stays far shorter than the library behind it (__TOTALDOCS__ documents synced).

Items kept by their highlights stay on the shelf at every setting: marking a passage is evidence of reading whatever the scroll position claims. The slider only governs the progress half of the bar.

Progress percentages on PDFs and videos are Readwise's scroll estimate, so 100% on a talk means the transcript reached the end, not that every minute was watched. Highlight counts are the harder evidence.
